OUR STORY

"Pastry is patience made visible — every layer a decision, every glaze a small act of care."

Maison Éclair began in a kitchen no larger than a closet, with one recipe passed down through three generations. Today, the ovens are bigger, but nothing else has changed — no shortcuts, no substitutes, just butter, time, and attention.
